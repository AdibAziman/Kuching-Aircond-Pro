## 1. Professional Logo Concept

### Conceptual Anatomy
The **Kuching Aircond Pro** brand mark unites three distinct visual metaphors:
1. **The Dynamic Aero-Fin:** Streamlined cooling airflow vanes angled at 45 degrees, evoking laminar thermodynamic efficiency.
2. **The Frost Crystal / Droplet Apex:** A geometric teardrop representing pure, dehumidified moisture extraction and crystal-clean chemical wash.
3. **The Shield of Certified Craftsmanship:** An underlying hexagonal structural boundary conveying CIDB-certified safety, warranty protection, and mechanical permanence.

```
       ▲  /\
      / \/  \     <- Dynamic Airflow Vane (Arctic Cyan #0EA5E9)
     |  /\  |     <- Pure Droplet Core (Frost Ice #E0F2FE)
      \/  \/      <- Mechanical Shield Foundation (Deep Navy #0F243A)
```

### Wordmark Lockup
- Primary Display: **KUCHING AIRCOND PRO**
- Weight Pair: "KUCHING AIRCOND" in *Plus Jakarta Sans Bold* (Weight 800), "PRO" in high-contrast *Arctic Cyan Badge Box* or vibrant accent styling.
- Sub-kicker: `Sarawak Certified HVAC Specialists · Est. 2018`

## 2. Brand Personality & Archetype

**Primary Archetype:** The Expert Craftsman (Everyday Hero)  
**Secondary Archetype:** The Transparent Neighbor (Local Sarawakian Ally)

| Brand Trait | How It Sounds | What It Never Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Technically Authoritative** | "We measure compressor running amps, delta-T temperature differential, and digital manifold PSI." | Never speaks in vague colloquialisms like "rasa sejuk lah sikit kot". |
| **Unflinchingly Honest** | "Your gas pressure is at 135 PSI—this is factory spec. You do NOT need a gas top-up." | Never invents fake problems or manipulates client ignorance. |
| **Respectful & Courteous** | "Good morning, Puan. Our team is laying down protective floor mats before we begin." | Never tracks mud, leaves debris, or speaks casually without permission. |
| **Crisp & Modern** | Clear digital job sheets, transparent prices, instant WhatsApp booking. | Never writes handwritten illegible scribbles on grease-stained cardboard. |

---

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines

- **Malaysian English Standard:** Professional, warm, and idiomatic to Malaysian professional contexts (e.g. "We service residential split units and commercial ceiling cassettes across Greater Kuching").
- **Language Code-Switching:** Friendly integration of familiar local terminology (*Inverter*, *Overhaul*, *Non-acidic chemical wash*, *SESCO bill savings*).
- **Zero Jargon Intimidation:** Explains technical issues with clear physical causes:
  - *Say:* "Your drain pan is blocked by bacterial bio-slime caused by Sarawak's high humidity. We will pressure-flush the pipe and sanitize the tray."
  - *Don't Say:* "Sistem tersumbat bang, kena cuci semua RM300."

## 6. Icon Style & Visual Language

- **Format:** Clean stroke geometry, 1.75px line weight, rounded stroke caps (`Lucide-React` standard).
- **Rule:** Never use decorative emoji as bullet points. All icons must denote literal functional affordances or technical mechanisms (e.g. `ThermometerSnowflake`, `ShieldCheck`, `Gauge`, `Clock3`, `Wrench`, `CheckCircle2`).

---

## 7. Photography & Visual Asset Direction

- **Anti-Slop Protocol:** No generic western stock photos of blonde families in winter jackets.
- **Real Sarawak Authenticity:** Imagery must reflect modern Malaysian homes (tiled floors, plaster ceilings, timber louvers) and authentic local commercial spaces (kopitiams, shophouses, modern cafes).
- **Technician Portrayal:** Technicians pictured in clean navy blue polos with the company emblem, wearing protective work equipment, holding real diagnostic tools (Yellow Jacket manifold gauge, Bosch cordless drill, pressure washer wand).
- **Before & After Proofs:** Authentic splits showcasing black mold buildup on evaporator blower rollers transitioning to factory-silver clean coils after alkaline foaming.
