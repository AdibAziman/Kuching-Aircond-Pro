## 1. Executive Summary & Company Story

Founded in 2018 by veteran mechanical engineering technician Ir. / Ts. Adrian Ting and his lead partner Khairul Anuar, **Kuching Aircond Pro** emerged in response to a glaring gap in the Sarawak air conditioning service sector: a lack of accountability, inconsistent pricing, and the notorious "freelancer disappearance" syndrome common among uncertified local contractors.

Operating from their central dispatch depot at Jalan Tun Jugah, Kuching, the business has grown from a single mobile service van into a dedicated fleet of 4 service vehicles and a tight-knit squad of **8 certified technicians** accredited by the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) and certified under Suruhanjaya Tenaga (Wireman Grade 2/3) and Department of Environment (DOE) for Refrigerant Handling (CFC/HFC/R32 Certification).

Kuching Aircond Pro specializes in residential, light commercial, and enterprise cooling solutions. Operating in Sarawak's equatorial, high-humidity climate (average 80–90% relative humidity, 31°C–34°C year-round temperature), air conditioning in Kuching is not a luxury—it is an indispensable public health and productivity utility. Biological slime (*bakteria berlendir*), black mold spores, and severe coil corrosion from high ambient humidity degrade cooling efficiency within 4 to 6 months of unserviced operation.

## 4. Deep-Dive Customer Pain Points in Kuching

Through customer interviews across Stampin, Batu Kawa, and Tabuan Tranquility, we identified the five acute customer anxieties:

1. **"The Gas Refill Trap" (Scam Gas Habis):** 84% of surveyed Kuching homeowners reported being told by previous technicians that their gas was completely empty, even when modern inverter units rarely lose gas without an active mechanical copper leak.
2. **Post-Service Water Leaking (Aircond Menitis Air):** The most common complaint is an aircond leaking water onto electronics, television consoles, or beds 48 to 72 hours after a cheap general cleaning, caused by failure to vacuum the drain pipe or improper re-seating of the evaporator drainage tray.
3. **Dirty Water Damage to Walls & Furnishings:** Unskilled technicians spraying chemical wash without high-grade waterproof shroud bags, staining expensive emulsion paint or soaking parquet/laminate flooring.
4. **Flaky Attendance & Late Arrivals:** Contractors promising "pagi bang" and arriving at 4:30 PM without notice, ruining the customer's work-from-home schedule or Saturday family plans.
5. **Skyrocketing SESCO Electricity Bills:** Aircond compressors straining against choked cooling coils and clogged blower wheels, consuming up to 35% more electricity every month.
