## 2. Deep-Dive Local Competitor Breakdown

### Competitor A: "Ah Meng Aircond Services" (Informal Freelancers)
- **Profile:** Unregistered single-operator or father-son team with a ladder on a Proton Wira / Hilux.
- **Strengths:** Dirt-cheap entry price advertised on Facebook groups ("Servis RM40!"). Fast same-day arrival if nearby.
- **Critical Weaknesses:**
  - Aggressive hidden fees: Always claims refrigerant is 0 PSI and charges RM120 for "gas top up".
  - Uses caustic industrial acid that strips the blue protective hydrophilic coating off the aluminum fins, causing permanent pinhole refrigerant leaks within 6 months.
  - No warranty, no digital invoice, disappears when unit starts leaking water.

### Competitor B: "Borneo Cool Engineering" (Traditional HVAC Contractor)
- **Profile:** Established 2005, operates a brick-and-mortar shophouse in Pending Industrial Estate. Focuses primarily on government tenders and central chiller installation.
- **Strengths:** Strong technical capacity for heavy industrial VRV/VRF systems; corporate billing capability.
- **Critical Weaknesses:**
  - Completely neglects residential customers: calls go to an indifferent receptionist who quotes "mungkin minggu depan".
  - Antiquated website with broken forms and zero mobile optimization.
  - No upfront pricing; demands an on-site RM80 "inspection fee" before quoting.

### Competitor C: "Peninsula App Services" (National Aggregator Apps)
- **Profile:** Digital gig-economy platforms advertising in Sarawak via Google Ads.
- **Strengths:** Polished mobile UI and automated payment gateways.
- **Critical Weaknesses:**
  - They do not employ technicians; they broker leads to whatever low-bidding freelance sub-contractor is signed up in Kuching.
  - Takes 20% to 30% cut, incentivizing the sub-contractor to cut corners on actual chemical soaking time.
  - Zero local Sarawak identity or accountability if disputes arise.

## 5. Local Customer Expectations in Sarawak

1. **WhatsApp Over Everything:** 92% of Sarawak consumers prefer initiating, confirming, and receiving photos through WhatsApp rather than filling anonymous web forms or making cold telephone calls.
2. **Speed of Response (Pantang Tunggu Lama):** A reply within 5 minutes on WhatsApp generates a 400% higher booking conversion rate than an email reply 4 hours later.
3. **Friendly, Respectful Manner (Budi Bahasa):** Technicians must communicate warmly in Sarawak Malay, standard BM, or Mandarin/Hokkien, taking off work shoes at the threshold and greeting the household respectfully.
4. **Instant Digital Payment:** Expectation of seamless cashless payment via DuitNow QR or Sarawak Pay (S Pay Global) directly upon job sign-off.
