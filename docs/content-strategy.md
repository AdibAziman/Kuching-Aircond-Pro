## 5. Comprehensive Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: How often should I service my aircond in Kuching's humid weather?**  
> *A: Due to Sarawak's high ambient humidity (80–90%), biological slime and mold multiply significantly faster than in drier climates. For bedroom units used 8 hours nightly, we recommend a General Service every 3 to 4 months, and a Chemical Wash every 9 to 12 months. For living rooms or high-traffic home offices, chemical wash is recommended every 6 months to maintain SESCO energy efficiency.*

**Q2: What is the difference between Chemical Wash and Chemical Overhaul?**  
> *A: A Chemical Wash is performed with the unit mounted on the wall using a protective shroud bag to treat the front coil, fan blower, and drain tray. A Chemical Overhaul involves fully unmounting the entire unit from the wall, dismantling every component, and soaking the coil in a chemical neutralization bath. Overhaul is recommended for units neglected for over a year or suffering from severe internal drainage blockage.*

**Q3: Will the chemical wash damage or corrode my aircond fins?**  
> *A: Absolutely not. Unscrupulous contractors use cheap industrial hydrochloric acid that etches aluminum and strips the blue protective coating. We exclusively formulate mild, non-acidic bio-alkaline coil foaming cleansers that dissolve organic grease and fungal mold while preserving the structural integrity of your aluminum fins.*

**Q4: Do you really cover Kota Samarahan and Batu Kawa without extra transport charges?**  
> *A: Yes, 100%. Kuching, Kota Samarahan, Batu Kawa, Petra Jaya, Matang, and Stampin are all within our primary operating radius. Our standard online pricing applies equally across all these areas with RM0 travel surcharge.*

**Q5: What does your 30-Day Workmanship Warranty cover?**
