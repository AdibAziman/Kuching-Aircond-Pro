# Customer Personas & Behavioral Profiles
**Client:** Kuching Aircond Pro  
**Market:** Kuching, Kota Samarahan, Batu Kawa, Petra Jaya, Matang, Stampin  
**Prepared By:** UX & Market Research Team  

---

## Persona 1: The Protective Homeowner
**Name:** Dayang Nurul Huda (Age 34)  
**Location:** Tabuan Tranquility, Kuching  
**Occupation:** Senior Executive, Sarawak State Civil Service  
**Household:** Married, 2 young children (ages 2 and 5), elderly mother-in-law  
**Equipment:** 4 split units (Daikin Inverter 1.0HP in bedrooms, Panasonic 2.0HP in living room)

### Demographics & Psychographics
- Values family health, indoor air quality, and punctuality.
- Busy professional schedule; work-from-home on alternating weekdays.
- Active on WhatsApp, Facebook parenting groups, and Google Maps reviews.

### Core Pain Points & Triggers
- Her 2-year-old toddler has nocturnal coughing fits caused by mold spores and dust accumulation in the master bedroom air conditioner.
- The living room unit has begun dripping water onto the television console.
- Dread of disreputable repairmen who arrive late, track mud on the floor, and leave dirty chemical residue on walls.

### Decision-Making Criteria
1. **Punctuality & Professional Etiquette:** Technicians must arrive within the agreed 1-hour window.
2. **Floor & Wall Protection:** Guaranteed mess-free service with protective mats.
3. **Written Warranty:** Certainty that if water leaks tomorrow, someone will take responsibility immediately.
4. **Instant WhatsApp Scheduling:** Wants to send photos of the unit and receive a fixed quotation in under 5 minutes.

## Persona 2: The High-Traffic F&B Operator
**Name:** Kelvin Tan (Age 42)  
**Location:** Gala City / Saradise Commercial Square, Kuching  
**Occupation:** Managing Partner, Artisan Kopitiam & Bakery  
**Equipment:** 3 Ceiling Cassette units (3.0HP Acson) + 2 Wall Mounted units in kitchen/prep area

### Demographics & Psychographics
- Fast-paced, margin-conscious entrepreneur whose business depends on customer comfort.
- Operates 7 days a week, 7:30 AM to 10:00 PM.
- If the dining hall air conditioner blows warm air during peak Sunday brunch, customer complaints surge and average table dwell time drops.

### Core Pain Points & Triggers
- Airborne cooking grease (*minyak masakan*) coats aircond filters and coils, turning into gummy sludge that chokes cooling airflow within 8 weeks.
- Heavy SESCO commercial tariff bills eating into food profit margins.
- Cannot shut down during operating hours; needs servicing at 6:30 AM or 10:00 PM.

### Decision-Making Criteria
1. **Off-Peak Servicing Flexibility:** Early morning or late evening service capability.
2. **Scheduled Preventive Maintenance Contracts (Quarterly):** Wants an automated maintenance schedule with SST e-invoices.
3. **Emergency 2-Hour Response:** Priority dispatch when a commercial unit breaks down mid-service.

## Persona 3: The Kota Samarahan Student Rental Landlord
**Name:** Jason Wong (Age 48)  
**Location:** Uni-Garden / Desa Ilmu, Kota Samarahan (Resides in Stampin, Kuching)  
**Occupation:** Private Investor & Property Manager  
**Equipment:** 12 units of 1.0HP split units across 3 apartments rented to UNIMAS & UiTM students

### Demographics & Psychographics
- Practical, volume-oriented landlord who manages multiple properties remotely.
- Wants zero headaches from tenant complaints about "aircond tak sejuk".
- Needs reliable service without paying inflated travel surcharges for Kota Samarahan.

### Core Pain Points & Triggers
- Students run air conditioners 14+ hours a day at 16°C with dirty filters, burning out capacitors and choking drainage tubes.
- Previous contractors charged an extra RM40 "transport fee" simply because the property is in Samarahan.
- Unreliable freelancers take cash from students, perform incomplete washes, and the tenant complains again 2 weeks later.

### Decision-Making Criteria
1. **Multi-Unit Volume Discount:** Clear tier pricing for servicing 3+ units in one visit (e.g., RM50/unit instead of RM60).
2. **Zero Samarahan Surcharge:** Explicit confirmation that Kota Samarahan is part of the standard coverage radius.
3. **Digital Report & Video Proof:** Wants before/after photos and WhatsApp job summary sent directly to his phone so he can pay via DuitNow QR.

## Persona 4: The Corporate Office Manager
**Name:** Grace Sim (Age 39)  
**Location:** Petra Jaya Government & Corporate Corridor, Kuching  
**Occupation:** Head of Administration & Facilities, Law Firm  
**Equipment:** 8 Ceiling Cassette units + Server Room dedicated 2.5HP split unit (runs 24/7)

### Demographics & Psychographics
- Responsible for office budget, operational uptime, and vendor compliance.
- Strict requirement for corporate documentation: Official Quotations, Purchase Orders (PO), CIDB-certified personnel, and e-Invoices with Sarawak SST details.

### Core Pain Points & Triggers
- Freelance repairmen cannot issue official tax invoices or corporate vendor receipts.
- Server room cooling failure poses immediate risk of server overheating and network downtime.
- Dirty air conditioning units causing sick building syndrome and absenteeism among associates.

### Decision-Making Criteria
1. **Corporate Compliance:** SSM registration number, CIDB Green Cards, official corporate billing.
2. **Server Room SLA:** Priority emergency coverage with guaranteed response times.
3. **Preventive Maintenance Contract:** Biannual coil chemical washing and monthly filter sanitization contracts with SLA report cards.

## Persona 5: The Value-Conscious Suburban Family
**Name:** Encik Rosli Bin Mansor (Age 45)  
**Location:** MJC Batu Kawa New Township / Matang Jaya  
**Occupation:** Secondary School Teacher (SMK)  
**Equipment:** 2 wall-mounted units (1.0HP York, 1.5HP Sharp)

### Demographics & Psychographics
- Family-first, budget-conscious household managing tight monthly expenses.
- Highly skeptical of contractors who upsell unnecessary gas top-ups.
- Prefers dealing via honest Malay/English WhatsApp conversations.

### Core Pain Points & Triggers
- Aircond blowing room-temperature air during hot dry months (May to September).
- Scared of getting hit with an unexpected RM250 bill when his budget is RM100.
- Needs clear explanation of why an overhaul is needed instead of a simple filter clean.

### Decision-Making Criteria
1. **Transparent Upfront Price Menu:** Wants to see the exact price before the technician steps into the house.
2. **No Pressure / No Hard Sell:** Technician explains issues politely without coercing him into expensive replacements.
3. **DuitNow / Sarawak Pay Accepted:** Seamless digital payment on completion.
