## 2. Sprint Timeline & Execution Phases

### Sprint 1: Discovery, Audit & Local Market Research (Week 1)
- Conduct deep interviews with founder Ts. Adrian Ting and lead technician Khairul Anuar.
- Competitor audit of 14 local Kuching contractors, identifying the ubiquitous "Gas Habis" scam and lack of written warranties.
- Customer journey mapping for 5 distinct local personas across Tabuan Tranquility, Gala City, Uni-Garden, and Batu Kawa.
- Formulation of the 50-keyword local SEO matrix.

### Sprint 2: Brand Identity, UX Wireframes & Copywriting (Week 2)
- Creation of the "Dynamic Aero-Fin & Crystal Shield" brand identity.
- Color system locked to Brand Navy (`#0F243A`), Arctic Cyan (`#0EA5E9`), and Clean Neutral (`#F8FAFC`).
- Production-ready copywriting produced for all 6 service categories, objection handling, and FAQ modules.
- Mobile-first wireframing focusing on the WhatsApp instant-conversion pathway.

### Sprint 3: Technical Engineering & Interactive Tools (Weeks 3 & 4)
- React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 8 scaffold configured with Tailwind CSS v4.
- Development of the Interactive Instant Price & Package Estimator with dynamic horsepower and multi-unit discount calculations.
- Implementation of the Before & After interactive touch slider for coil restoration proof.
- Development of the Certified Technician Credential Registry (CIDB & Wireman verification).
- Integration of Schema.org `HVACBusiness` JSON-LD structured data and OpenGraph cards.

### Sprint 4: Testing, Quality Assurance & CWV Optimization (Week 5)
- WCAG AA accessibility audit (contrast compliance, keyboard focus navigation, touch targets $\ge 44\text{px}$).
- Mobile responsive testing across iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, and iPad viewport profiles.
- Zero layout shift (CLS = 0.00) optimization and asset lightweighting.
- End-to-end testing of WhatsApp message pre-fill strings and booking modal validation.

### Sprint 5: Go-Live, Google Business Profile & Handover (Week 6)
- Production build deployment on high-speed CDN edge infrastructure.
- Google Search Console submission and XML sitemap indexing.
- Google Business Profile optimization with exact NAP consistency at Jalan Tun Jugah, Kuching.
- Delivery of the full 9-document agency strategy package to client executive leadership.
